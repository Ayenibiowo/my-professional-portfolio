PROJECT OVERVIEW

A clearer experience across connected workflows.

Healthcare workflows connect patient records, clinical operations, appointments, and care delivery. This project explores a clearer experience across those connected needs.

Where clarity matters most.

Simplifying patient records, appointments, and clinical operations in one connected experience.

The following design considerations describe the project’s focus. They are not presented as findings from completed user research.

Clinical coordination

Connected experience
