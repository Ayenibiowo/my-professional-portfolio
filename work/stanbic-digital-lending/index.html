THE CONTEXT

Making complexity
easier to navigate.

Digital lending connects several important moments: understanding an offer, checking eligibility, completing an application, and following an approval decision. This project focuses on making that journey clearer and more accessible.

EXPERIENCE PRIORITIES

A thoughtful approach
to connected experiences.

01

Clear applications

Structure the application journey so customers can understand what is needed at each step.

02

Eligibility and decisions

Make eligibility information and application progress easier to understand.

03

Connected customer experience

Consider the transitions between application, review, and approval as one journey.
