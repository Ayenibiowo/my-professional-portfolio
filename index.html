A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curiosity drives my thinking.
Purpose shapes my work.

I’m Oluwaseun Ayeni, a product design leader, strategist, founder, author, and mentor. My journey spans enterprise platforms, financial services, healthcare systems, digital marketplaces, and emerging technology.

I enjoy working where complexity meets possibility: bringing structure to ambiguous problems, connecting teams around shared goals, and creating experiences that work for people and businesses. Beyond design, I build ventures, mentor designers, and write about personal and professional growth.
